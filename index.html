The Artist

Get To Know
Big Shizzy.

Big Shizzy, also known as Toluwani, is an artist and entertainer from Ogun State, Nigeria.

His creative interests extend across music, entertainment, barbing and painting. These different areas form part of his identity as a multi-disciplinary creative.

Beyond his personal creative work, Big Shizzy is developing a professional platform that supports artists, brands and entertainment projects through promotion, branding and media services.

His vision is centred around creativity, entertainment and building a recognizable professional brand.

“Creativity is not limited to one direction. Big Shizzy's creative identity brings together music, entertainment, grooming, visual creativity and media.”
